// how we operate

the four-act roadmap.

Every project travels the same road, from a sharp strategic truth to a brand that is live everywhere.

ACT 01

discover

DAYS 1 – 7

Founder interrogation, customer interviews, competitor teardowns, and identifying the single sharp insight the brand will own.

ACT 02

define

DAYS 8 – 14

Formulating the strategic thesis, market positioning, messaging hierarchy, and two contrasting aesthetic directions.

ACT 03

create

DAYS 15 – 35

Building the full system: marks, typography, colour, brand applications and screen prototypes.

ACT 04

grow

DAYS 36 & BEYOND

Rollout: brand guidelines, launch assets and developer handoff, with a check on every live touchpoint.
