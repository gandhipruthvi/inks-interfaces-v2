// the challenge

Twenty years of teaching, finally given a name.

What needed to change

Twenty years, no brand: Proovi had built a daily practice that drew students from four cities. There was no name above the door — just her phone number on a WhatsApp broadcast. | Online and in-studio, two faces: The in-person students and the online students were getting two different experiences. The brand had to hold both without splintering. | Tutorial library needed a brand: Selling pre-recorded yoga online is a crowded category. Proovi&#39;s twenty years of teaching is the differentiator — the brand had to surface that, not the platform. | Teacher-led, not studio-led: Most yoga brands are about the room. Trivid had to be about the teacher — without becoming a personality brand.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

Teacher-first: Every brand surface leads with Proovi — not the room, not the style, not the lineage. The teacher is the differentiator. | No lotus, no sanskrit: The whole brand drops the category&#39;s default visual vocabulary. Type, colour, paper — that&#39;s the system. | Same face, two surfaces: The in-studio collateral and the online tutorial brand share one wordmark, one palette, one rhythm. | Daily, not aspirational: The brand is built around daily practice — not a weekend retreat. Voice is consistent, calm, ongoing.

// strategic thesis

The teacher is the brand. Twenty years of teaching is the proof.
