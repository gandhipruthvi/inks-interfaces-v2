// the challenge

Twenty years of teaching, finally given a name.

What needed to change

  • Twenty years, no brandProovi had built a daily practice that drew students from four cities. There was no name above the door — just her phone number on a WhatsApp broadcast.
  • Online and in-studio, two facesThe in-person students and the online students were getting two different experiences. The brand had to hold both without splintering.
  • Tutorial library needed a brandSelling pre-recorded yoga online is a crowded category. Proovi's twenty years of teaching is the differentiator — the brand had to surface that, not the platform.
  • Teacher-led, not studio-ledMost yoga brands are about the room. Trivid had to be about the teacher — without becoming a personality brand.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

  • Teacher-firstEvery brand surface leads with Proovi — not the room, not the style, not the lineage. The teacher is the differentiator.
  • No lotus, no sanskritThe whole brand drops the category's default visual vocabulary. Type, colour, paper — that's the system.
  • Same face, two surfacesThe in-studio collateral and the online tutorial brand share one wordmark, one palette, one rhythm.
  • Daily, not aspirationalThe brand is built around daily practice — not a weekend retreat. Voice is consistent, calm, ongoing.

// strategic thesis

The teacher is the brand. Twenty years of teaching is the proof.
