// the challenge

From juice bar to destination café.

What needed to change

  • Three locations, three brandsEach café had been opened in a hurry. Every signboard, takeaway cup, and Instagram grid told a slightly different story.
  • Mistaken for a juice barDrop-in customers ordered juice and left. The coffee program — the actual business — was invisible from the street.
  • Forgotten on InstagramNo one geo-tagged Zestee. The interiors were beautiful but the brand never asked to be photographed.
  • No system, no scaleOpening café four would mean rebuilding café one from scratch. There was no kit of parts to copy.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

  • Warmth, not luxuryTerracotta and cream beat black and gold. Zestee should feel like a friend's living room, not a hotel lobby.
  • Hand over machineHand-painted signage. Hand-stamped sleeves. The brand wears its making process.
  • Type-ledA single custom display face does the heavy lifting. No stock fonts, no decorative flourish.
  • PhotographableEvery cup, plate, and sticker is designed first as a flat-lay. The brand earns its Instagram.

// strategic thesis

Built to be photographed. Designed to be returned to.

Front-of-pack clarity

02

Cohesive omnichannel system

From screen taps to shipping box unboxing, the identity remains unmistakably unified.
