// the challenge

Real cocoa, real vanilla, and a name parents can say.

What needed to change

  • Priced like a home bakerBrownies sold at ₹200 to 300 while the founder aimed for ₹500 to 600, and premium cakes beyond that.
  • No name yetIt had to be one real word, easy for parents to pronounce, and start with N or Y.
  • A crowded shelfLocal dessert brands lean on sweet-tooth names. Anything that sounded like a category word would disappear among them.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

  • Story-first namingA real word with meaning behind it, in the spirit of Theobroma, not a word that describes dessert.
  • Friendly luxuryBold red and butter cream with a confident wordmark. Premium enough for a gift box, warm enough for a child's birthday.
  • A character to carry itA mascot and pose library that works on packaging, stickers, menus and walls without a new illustration every time.

// strategic thesis

Yellow Pod: a dessert house built on honest ingredients, with a character who makes every box feel like a gift.
