// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

  • Joyful ColorsA palette that makes you smile.
  • Clean LayoutClear nutritional info without clutter.

// strategic thesis

Satori Bites brings the authentic flavors of Asian dimsum and noodle bars to a healthy snack format. We built a brand that balances the joy of an Asian treat with the cleanliness of health food.
