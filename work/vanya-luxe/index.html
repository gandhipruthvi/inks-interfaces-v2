// the challenge

Vanya Luxe.

What needed to change

Balancing Act: The brand needed to communicate clinical science without looking like medicine. | Tactile Digital: How do you convey the texture of a luxury cream through a website? | Information Hierarchy: Customers need to know active ingredients, but too much text breaks the luxury aesthetic. | Packaging Constraints: Sustainable materials had to feel heavy and premium.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

Whitespace as Luxury: Letting the products breathe on the page. | Macro Photography: Showing the exact texture of the product. | Clear Typography: Elegant serifs paired with highly legible sans-serifs for ingredients. | Frictionless UX: A checkout process that feels effortless.

// strategic thesis

Vanya Luxe brings clinical efficacy to the luxury skincare space. We created a digital experience and packaging system that feels as refined as the formulas themselves.
