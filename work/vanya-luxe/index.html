// the challenge

Vanya Luxe.

What needed to change

  • Balancing ActThe brand needed to communicate clinical science without looking like medicine.
  • Tactile DigitalHow do you convey the texture of a luxury cream through a website?
  • Information HierarchyCustomers need to know active ingredients, but too much text breaks the luxury aesthetic.
  • Packaging ConstraintsSustainable materials had to feel heavy and premium.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

  • Whitespace as LuxuryLetting the products breathe on the page.
  • Macro PhotographyShowing the exact texture of the product.
  • Clear TypographyElegant serifs paired with highly legible sans-serifs for ingredients.
  • Frictionless UXA checkout process that feels effortless.

// strategic thesis

Vanya Luxe brings clinical efficacy to the luxury skincare space. We created a digital experience and packaging system that feels as refined as the formulas themselves.
