// the challenge

Caffeine, lemonade, and an exclamation mark.

What needed to change

  • Energy drinks look like energy drinksThe category is a wall of black cans and neon angles. Hard to stand out by joining the chorus.
  • Lemonade looks like a kids' drinkPastel pink, hand-drawn lemons, summer-camp lettering. Not the brief either.
  • Three formats, one faceA can, a glass bottle, and a squeeze pouch — each one wants to look like a different brand by default.
  • Caffeine credibilityThe brand has to whisper 'this works' without writing it on the can in screaming yellow.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

  • Lemon-first colourThe brand's green is the colour of an under-ripe lemon — not the cliché yellow.
  • Confident type, no shoutingA single condensed sans does all the typography. No display flourishes, no italics.
  • Photography over illustrationEvery brand asset is a real product, real ice, real water. The drink earns its photo.
  • Format-modularCan, bottle, squeeze — same wordmark, same proportion, three completely different containers.

// strategic thesis

Lemon. Caffeine. An exclamation mark — earned, not added.
