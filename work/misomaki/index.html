// the challenge

Misomaki.

What needed to change

  • Saturated MarketSushi branding is notoriously repetitive. We needed a system that stood out without feeling alien.
  • Scalable PackagingTakeaway packaging had to be photogenic, sustainable, and functional across multiple container shapes.
  • Digital FirstThe brand needed to live primarily on screens before the physical restaurants opened.
  • Premium yet AccessibleThe aesthetic had to convey high-quality ingredients without intimidating everyday lunch customers.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

  • Bold TypographyA custom wordmark that commands attention without being illegible.
  • Stark ContrastHigh-contrast palettes that make the food photography pop.
  • Minimal NoiseRemoving every unnecessary element from the packaging.
  • Digital NativeDesigned for screens first, print second.

// strategic thesis

Misomaki redefines the modern sushi experience. In a market flooded with traditional imagery, we built a visual identity that is sharp, contemporary, and unapologetically bold.
