// the challenge

A fitness app that adapts to you, with a mark that moves like it.

What needed to change

  • A side project with no faceAzonix generates training plans from Apple Health data, but it was close to App Store launch without a logo, icon or splash screen.
  • AI tools fell shortThe founder tried generating the brand himself and kept landing on generic letterforms.
  • Gamified was the wrong signalThe app is built for a regular gym-goer in his thirties, not a streak-chasing audience. It had to feel bold and professional, not playful.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

  • Bold, not loudNike-level confidence with rounded edges only. No chrome, no dated shading.
  • One accentWhite, graphite and a single orange. Red and green stay reserved for in-app progress metrics.
  • The symbol does the workThe inner ribbon of the icon doubles as the app loader, filling from silver to orange while content loads.

// strategic thesis

A ribbon mark that rises like a training curve, then becomes the loader inside the app.
