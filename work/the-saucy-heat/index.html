// the challenge

A chilli oil that prefers a long, slow burn.

What needed to change

The category screams: Every chilli oil shelf is red flames, neon type, and exclamation marks. Standing out by joining the noise is impossible. | Quiet has to read as confident, not bland: Mute the noise and you risk looking like a polite condiment. The brand has to feel restrained AND serious about heat. | Bottle and tube, same brand: Bottle for kitchen, squeeze tube for picnic. The system has to feel like one brand across two very different containers. | Heat levels without scoville bars: Buyers want to know how spicy. The brand can&#39;t use the lazy pepper-icon scale everyone else uses.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

Dark over bright: The whole brand sits on a near-black background. The chilli oil is the only red in the frame. | Two heat levels, named: Original and Numbing. No scales, no flames. The names do the work icons can&#39;t. | Bottle and tube, same wordmark: Identical wordmark at three sizes. Two containers, one face. | Photography in low light: Every brand asset shot under a single warm light. The oil is the brightest thing in every frame.

// strategic thesis

The condiment your kitchen earns, not the one your shelf shouts about.
