// the challenge

A chilli oil that prefers a long, slow burn.

What needed to change

  • The category screamsEvery chilli oil shelf is red flames, neon type, and exclamation marks. Standing out by joining the noise is impossible.
  • Quiet has to read as confident, not blandMute the noise and you risk looking like a polite condiment. The brand has to feel restrained AND serious about heat.
  • Bottle and tube, same brandBottle for kitchen, squeeze tube for picnic. The system has to feel like one brand across two very different containers.
  • Heat levels without scoville barsBuyers want to know how spicy. The brand can't use the lazy pepper-icon scale everyone else uses.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

  • Dark over brightThe whole brand sits on a near-black background. The chilli oil is the only red in the frame.
  • Two heat levels, namedOriginal and Numbing. No scales, no flames. The names do the work icons can't.
  • Bottle and tube, same wordmarkIdentical wordmark at three sizes. Two containers, one face.
  • Photography in low lightEvery brand asset shot under a single warm light. The oil is the brightest thing in every frame.

// strategic thesis

The condiment your kitchen earns, not the one your shelf shouts about.
