// the challenge

Go Miles.

What needed to change

  • VisibilityVehicles needed to be instantly recognizable in heavy traffic.
  • TrustThe brand had to convey security and reliability for high-value logistics.
  • UniformityApparel had to be highly functional for drivers while maintaining brand aesthetics.
  • ScalabilityThe visual system had to work on an app icon and a 16-wheeler.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

  • High VisibilityUsing high-contrast colors for safety and recognition.
  • Motion ForwardTypography and iconography that suggest forward momentum.
  • Functional ApparelDesigning driver gear that is actually comfortable to work in.
  • Clear HierarchyEnsuring the logo is readable from a distance at high speeds.

// strategic thesis

Go Miles is rethinking urban mobility and logistics. We built a visual identity that is dynamic, reliable, and built for speed.
