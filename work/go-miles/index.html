// the challenge

Go Miles.

What needed to change

Visibility: Vehicles needed to be instantly recognizable in heavy traffic. | Trust: The brand had to convey security and reliability for high-value logistics. | Uniformity: Apparel had to be highly functional for drivers while maintaining brand aesthetics. | Scalability: The visual system had to work on an app icon and a 16-wheeler.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

High Visibility: Using high-contrast colors for safety and recognition. | Motion Forward: Typography and iconography that suggest forward momentum. | Functional Apparel: Designing driver gear that is actually comfortable to work in. | Clear Hierarchy: Ensuring the logo is readable from a distance at high speeds.

// strategic thesis

Go Miles is rethinking urban mobility and logistics. We built a visual identity that is dynamic, reliable, and built for speed.
