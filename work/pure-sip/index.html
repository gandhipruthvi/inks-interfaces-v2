// the challenge

Pure Sip.

What needed to change

Invisible Product: Water has no color or texture. The packaging must do all the communicating. | Visual Impact: Designing a packaging system that feels premium while remaining incredibly accessible. | Shelf Impact: Standing out in the most crowded aisle in the grocery store.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

Fluid Typography: Custom lettering that feels like flowing water. | Negative Space: Vast areas of white and blue to imply purity. | Cool Tones: A strict palette of blues and icy silvers.

// strategic thesis

Pure Sip is redefining everyday hydration. We designed a packaging system that feels refreshing, clean, and impossibly crisp.

three takeaways from this build.

Front-of-pack clarity

State the honest truth before the branding. Customers buy what they understand.

02

Subtle textures, weight, and finishes tell the customer you care about detail everywhere.

03

Cohesive omnichannel system

From screen taps to shipping box unboxing, the identity remains unmistakably unified.
