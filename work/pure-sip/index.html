// the challenge

Pure Sip.

What needed to change

  • Invisible ProductWater has no color or texture. The packaging must do all the communicating.
  • Visual ImpactDesigning a packaging system that feels premium while remaining incredibly accessible.
  • Shelf ImpactStanding out in the most crowded aisle in the grocery store.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

  • Fluid TypographyCustom lettering that feels like flowing water.
  • Negative SpaceVast areas of white and blue to imply purity.
  • Cool TonesA strict palette of blues and icy silvers.

// strategic thesis

Pure Sip is redefining everyday hydration. We designed a packaging system that feels refreshing, clean, and impossibly crisp.
