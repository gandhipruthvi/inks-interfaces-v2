// the challenge

Woven Wax.

What needed to change

  • Shelf PresenceStanding out in boutique stores surrounded by minimalist competitors.
  • Scent TranslationUsing color and typography to evoke specific fragrances.
  • Production CostsDesigning premium labels that remain cost-effective for small-batch runs.
  • GiftabilityThe unboxing experience had to feel gift-ready without extra wrapping.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

  • Textural GraphicsUsing visual noise and grain to imply physical texture.
  • Color as ScentAbstract color gradients mapping to fragrance notes.
  • Modular LabelsA system that easily adapts to new seasonal releases.
  • Warm TypographyFriendly, slightly nostalgic serif fonts.

// strategic thesis

Woven Wax creates artisanal, hand-poured candles. We developed a packaging system that feels as layered and complex as their signature scents.
