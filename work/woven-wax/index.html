// the challenge

Woven Wax.

What needed to change

Shelf Presence: Standing out in boutique stores surrounded by minimalist competitors. | Scent Translation: Using color and typography to evoke specific fragrances. | Production Costs: Designing premium labels that remain cost-effective for small-batch runs. | Giftability: The unboxing experience had to feel gift-ready without extra wrapping.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

Textural Graphics: Using visual noise and grain to imply physical texture. | Color as Scent: Abstract color gradients mapping to fragrance notes. | Modular Labels: A system that easily adapts to new seasonal releases. | Warm Typography: Friendly, slightly nostalgic serif fonts.

// strategic thesis

Woven Wax creates artisanal, hand-poured candles. We developed a packaging system that feels as layered and complex as their signature scents.

Front-of-pack clarity

State the honest truth before the branding. Customers buy what they understand.

02

Subtle textures, weight, and finishes tell the customer you care about detail everywhere.

03

Cohesive omnichannel system

From screen taps to shipping box unboxing, the identity remains unmistakably unified.
