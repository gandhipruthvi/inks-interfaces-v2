// the challenge

Evoque.

What needed to change

  • The Sea of SamenessFragrance brands often rely on the same tropes. Evoque needed a distinct attitude.
  • Visualizing ScentCommunicating complex scent profiles through a minimalist visual language.
  • PackagingBottle designs and boxes needed to be beautiful enough to display permanently.
  • Social ImpactCreating a visual language that stands out endlessly scrolling feeds.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

  • Raw AestheticsEmbracing imperfections in photography and layouts.
  • Bold Sans-SerifA brutalist approach to typography.
  • Monochrome CanvasLetting the scent and bottle design dictate the mood of the season.
  • Tactile PackagingUsing thick, textured cardstock and heavyweight glass bottles.

// strategic thesis

Evoque is a luxury fragrance house redefining modern scents. We created an identity that is evocative, minimal, and fiercely independent.
