// the challenge

Evoque.

What needed to change

The Sea of Sameness: Fragrance brands often rely on the same tropes. Evoque needed a distinct attitude. | Visualizing Scent: Communicating complex scent profiles through a minimalist visual language. | Packaging: Bottle designs and boxes needed to be beautiful enough to display permanently. | Social Impact: Creating a visual language that stands out endlessly scrolling feeds.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

Raw Aesthetics: Embracing imperfections in photography and layouts. | Bold Sans-Serif: A brutalist approach to typography. | Monochrome Canvas: Letting the scent and bottle design dictate the mood of the season. | Tactile Packaging: Using thick, textured cardstock and heavyweight glass bottles.

// strategic thesis

Evoque is a luxury fragrance house redefining modern scents. We created an identity that is evocative, minimal, and fiercely independent.
