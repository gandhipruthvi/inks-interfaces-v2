// the challenge

A travel booking habit, mapped to your day.

What needed to change

  • Opened on holidays, ignored on TuesdaysUsers only thought about Globetap when travelling. The product was a holiday utility, not a daily one.
  • Stations without contextThe map showed stations. It didn't show whether one was on your usual route, or whether it would save you time.
  • No reason to returnThe product had no progress, no streak, no signal that using it mattered.
  • Onboarding was a search barDay-one users were dropped onto a map and told to figure it out. 60% never came back.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

  • Bottle-firstThe first screen isn't a map — it's a bottle. The product starts where the behaviour starts.
  • Route-awareStations are filtered against the user's actual commute, not the radius around their phone.
  • Streak as feedbackEvery refill logged builds a streak. Streak loss is the only motivator stronger than convenience.
  • One-thumb defaultEvery primary action sits in the lower third of the screen. The app works on a moving train.

// strategic thesis

Design the bottle into the day, and the app follows.
