// the challenge

A travel booking habit, mapped to your day.

What needed to change

Opened on holidays, ignored on Tuesdays: Users only thought about Globetap when travelling. The product was a holiday utility, not a daily one. | Stations without context: The map showed stations. It didn&#39;t show whether one was on your usual route, or whether it would save you time. | No reason to return: The product had no progress, no streak, no signal that using it mattered. | Onboarding was a search bar: Day-one users were dropped onto a map and told to figure it out. 60% never came back.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

Bottle-first: The first screen isn&#39;t a map — it&#39;s a bottle. The product starts where the behaviour starts. | Route-aware: Stations are filtered against the user&#39;s actual commute, not the radius around their phone. | Streak as feedback: Every refill logged builds a streak. Streak loss is the only motivator stronger than convenience. | One-thumb default: Every primary action sits in the lower third of the screen. The app works on a moving train.

// strategic thesis

Design the bottle into the day, and the app follows.
