// the challenge

Hardin Jewels.

What needed to change

  • Aging DemographicThe brand needed to attract younger buyers without alienating their loyal base.
  • Unboxing ExperienceThe physical packaging needed to feel as valuable as the jewelry inside.
  • Visual ClutterPrevious marketing was too busy. The pieces needed room to breathe.
  • Digital TranslationEnsuring the sparkle and detail translated perfectly to mobile screens.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

  • Editorial FramingTreating every product shot like a fashion editorial.
  • Tactile PackagingHeavy stocks, blind embossing, and velvet interiors.
  • Restrained PaletteLetting the gold and gemstones provide the color.
  • Modern SerifA custom logotype that bridges classic and contemporary.

// strategic thesis

Hardin Jewels offers contemporary fine jewelry. We created a refined visual system anchored by a delicate monogram and subdued color palette to let the fine jewelry take center stage.
