// the challenge

A playful rebrand for a beloved playschool.

What needed to change

  • Designed for admin, used by parentEvery flow assumed an admin user. Parents had to navigate seventeen screens to find a single piece of information.
  • WhatsApp was winningParents stayed on WhatsApp because it was faster. The app had to feel as fast as a message, not as slow as a portal.
  • Pickup-line UXThe app's heaviest use happens one-handed, in a car, at 2:50pm. The product had to be designed for that exact moment.
  • Four campuses, one appEach campus had different schedules, fee structures, and teachers. The product couldn't fragment by location.

// strategic positioning

Designing for the human decision maker, not an algorithm.

  • One screen, three answersThe parent home shows today's update, today's pickup time, and today's fee status. Three answers, no taps.
  • Faster than WhatsAppEvery common action — pay, view, message teacher — takes under fifteen seconds. The app has to outrun the chat group.
  • Big targets, low-lightAll tap targets sized for one-handed use in low light. The app is opened in cars, not at desks.
  • Joyful, not childishBright primary colour palette. Playful illustration. The brand is for the kid; the UX is for the adult.

// strategic thesis

Design for the moments that matter, from the classroom to the playground.
